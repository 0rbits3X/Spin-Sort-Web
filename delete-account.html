Spin Sort: Request data deletion

App by ROBOROYGAMESSTUDIO.

Option 1: inside the game

Open Spin Sort > Menu > CLOUD SAVE > DELETE MY DATA (tap twice). This deletes your cloud save and your account.

Option 2: by email

Email REPLACE_WITH_YOUR_EMAIL with the subject "Delete my Spin Sort data". If you signed in with Google, write from that Google account email address. We delete your account and cloud save within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

Your anonymous account, your email address (if you added one) and your saved progress (level, coins, skins). Data kept on your phone is removed when you uninstall the game. Ad data collected by Unity Ads is handled by Unity: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy.
