Spin Sort: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-25. App by ROBOROYGAMESSTUDIO ("we"). Contact: spinsortsupport@gmail.com

What stays on your phone

Your level, coins, skins and settings are saved on your phone only (unless you turn on cloud save, see below). We do not receive them.

Ads (Unity Ads)

The game shows ads through Unity Ads. Unity and its ad partners may collect your device advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device model, operating system, app version and ad interaction data, to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. At first start we ask if you want personalized ads. You can choose "Basic ads only" and change your choice any time in the game menu (PRIVACY). See Unity's privacy policy: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy.

Optional cloud save (Supabase)

If cloud save is turned on in the game, we create an anonymous account and store your progress (level, coins, skins) on Supabase servers. If you choose "Continue with Google" we also receive and store your Google account email address and a user ID from Google (we never see your Google password). We use this only to keep and restore your progress. We do not sell or share it. Data is sent over HTTPS.

Deleting your data

In the game: Menu > CLOUD SAVE > DELETE MY DATA deletes your cloud save and account. Or ask us: see how to request deletion. We delete cloud data within 30 days of a request. Uninstalling the game removes the data on your phone.

Children

Spin Sort is not made for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy we update the date above.
